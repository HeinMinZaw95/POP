Key Concept & Equation

Centripetal force is the net force directed toward the center of the circular path.

\( F_c = \frac{m v^2}{r} \)

\( F_c = m \omega^2 r \)

Fc = Centripetal Force | m = Mass | v = Velocity | r = Radius
