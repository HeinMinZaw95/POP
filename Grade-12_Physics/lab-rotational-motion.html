Key Concept & Equation

Centripetal acceleration is proportional to the square of angular velocity and the radius.

\( \omega = \frac{2\pi \times \text{RPM}}{60} \)

\( a_c = \omega^2 r \)

ω = Angular Velocity | r = Sample Radius | ac = Centripetal Acceleration
