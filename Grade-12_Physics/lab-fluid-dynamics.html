Key Concept & Equation

Bernoulli's principle states that faster moving air creates lower pressure, producing upward lift.

\( P + \frac{1}{2}\rho v^2 + \rho gh = \text{constant} \)

\( F_{\text{lift}} = \frac{1}{2} C_L \rho v^2 S \)

P = Pressure | ρ = Air Density | v = Velocity | F = Lift Force
